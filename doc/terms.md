## 2. Educational & Mathematical Demonstration Only — No Medical Use

The CAT Scan Demonstration is an interactive educational simulation designed
to illustrate the mathematics of computed tomography, specifically the Radon
Transform, sinograms, ray projections, and Frank Natterer's Filtered
Backprojection (FBP) algorithm:

* **NO MEDICAL OR CLINICAL USE:** THE APPLICATION IS STRICTLY AN ACADEMIC
  MATHEMATICAL VISUALIZATION. IT IS **NOT** A MEDICAL DEVICE, IS **NOT**
  FDA-CLEARED, AND MUST **NEVER** BE USED FOR MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS, PATIENT CARE,
  RADIATION DOSIMETRY, ONCOLOGY, RADIOLOGICAL EVALUATION, OR CLINICAL
  DECISION-MAKING.

* Synthetic phantoms (e.g. Shepp-Logan phantom variants) are mathematical test
  functions and do not depict actual patient anatomy or pathology.
